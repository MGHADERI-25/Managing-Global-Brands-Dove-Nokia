# Managing Global Brands — Dove Brand Audit and Nokia Brand Analysis

**Course:** Managing Global Brands  
**Institution:** International Business School (IBS), Budapest

---

## Project Overview

This repository contains academic coursework completed for the *Managing Global Brands* course. The project focuses on strategic brand management concepts through a comprehensive Dove brand audit and a series of Nokia brand analysis presentations.

The work applies major branding frameworks including:
- Customer-Based Brand Equity (CBBE)
- Brand positioning
- Points of parity and points of difference
- Brand resonance
- Brand identity systems
- Integrated marketing communication (IMC)
- Brand audit methodology

---

## Main Assessment — Dove Brand Audit

The Dove brand audit evaluates:
- Brand equity
- Brand positioning
- Communication strategy
- Brand associations
- Brand identity
- Consumer perception
- Competitive differentiation

The analysis applies frameworks from:
- Kevin Lane Keller
- David Aaker
- Jean-Noël Kapferer

---

## Nokia Brand Analysis Presentations

The repository also includes four presentation topics analyzing Nokia:

1. Brand history — what makes Nokia unique
2. Brand elements — analysis of visual and strategic brand elements
3. Positioning — value proposition and market positioning
4. Communication — how communication builds brand equity
